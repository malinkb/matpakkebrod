# Changelog

All notable tested changes to Matpakkebrød will be documented here.

## v0.1 — Initial dry-yeast prototype

Status: **baked and tested**

Initial prototype using wheat flour, rye flour, Guinness, honey, rapeseed oil and dry yeast.

Key characteristics:

- one loaf
- baked in a metal loaf tin approximately 31.7 × 10.8 × 6 cm
- rye scald used to bind moisture
- target core temperature approximately 95–96 °C
- designed for a soft, sliceable and filling lunchbox-style loaf

Observed issue:

- bread became noticeably dry after being stored from approximately 16 October to 19 October 2026 in a bread bag in the refrigerator

The next experiment keeps the recipe essentially unchanged while changing the storage protocol, to test whether refrigeration rather than the formula was the main cause of staling.
